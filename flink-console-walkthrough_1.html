Practice: spot the poison pill

One of these records will break a naive deserializer. Which one, and why?
{"ticker": "AMZN", "price": 231.55, "event_time": "2026-09-02T10:01:02Z"}
{"ticker": "MSFT", "price": 512.10, "event_time": "2026-09-02T10:01:03Z"}
{"ticker": "GOOG", "price": "n/a", event_time: 2026-09-02T10:01:04Z}
{"ticker": "AAPL", "price": 228.90, "event_time": "2026-09-02T10:01:05Z"}
Reveal answer
The third record: price is a string instead of a number, and the field name event_time is missing its quotes — invalid JSON. A naive deserializer throws on this and can stall the whole task.

The fix pattern: catch deserialization failures and route the bad record to a side output (a "dead-letter" stream) instead of letting it crash the job — the good records keep flowing.
